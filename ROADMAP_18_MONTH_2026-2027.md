## 5 strategic pillars

| # | Pillar | Why It Matters |

| 1 | **Regulatory First-Mover** | Every major regulation (EU AI Act, DORA, NIS2, CRA, UK AI Bill) creates a demand spike. MEOK must have MCP servers live BEFORE enforcement deadlines. |
| 2 | **MCP Security Authority** | 35,000+ MCP servers, 30+ CVEs, zero governance standards. MEOK publishes the standards and certifies the servers. |

| 4 | **Industry Vertical Packs** | Bundling MCPs by industry (Financial Services, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Legal) increases deal size and reduces churn. |
| 5 | **Standards Body Positioning** | The company that writes the MCP governance standard becomes the default governance layer for the entire ecosystem. |

## What "launching on the EU AI Act deadline wave" looks like (the urgency math)

From 8 Jun 2026 (T-55 days to EU AI Act Aug 2):
- 50,000 EU enterprises with high-risk AI use cases
- $35M or 7% global turnover penalty = existential risk for non-compliance
- Free EU AI Act risk scanner at meok.ai/scan = top-of-funnel
- EU AI Act compliance as a wedge into SOV3 Cloud Business tier ($49/user/mo)

The Q3 LAUNCH is structured so that the EU AI Act Aug 2 deadline is the **demand spike** that pulls users into the funnel. The 5,000 free scanner completions target is the top-of-funnel metric; the 5% conversion to SOV3 Cloud Team tier is the $50K MRR math.

## 6 strategic bets (what could go wrong)

| Bet | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| EU AI Act Aug 2 deadline drives demand | Digital Omnibus extends deadline | Plan for Aug 2026; treat extension as margin of safety (per audit) |
| MCP marketplace dominance (6/6 channels) | Marketplace aggregators die or pivot | Direct-to-developer via npm + Docker Hub as the durable channel |
| Industry pack pricing ($15K-$500K) | Enterprises won't pay for "MCP layer" | Lead with free scanner → freemium MCPs → paid pack; pricing validated by pilots |
| ISO 42001 cert program (MEOK as body) | Standards body legitimacy requires multi-stakeholder | Partner with Big 4 audit firm; anchor on existing CSOAI brand |
| SOV3 Cloud (MongoDB play) | Cloud-provider competition (AWS Bedrock, etc.) | Lead with regulatory-specialized UX (compliance dashboard), not generic hosting |
| ark-forge parity | ark-forge raises + adds frameworks | Maintain 25+ framework lead; attack via multi-jurisdiction (US/EU/CN/JP/AU) |

## What's NOT in the 18-month plan (the explicit non-goals)

- **Not a CRM / GRC platform replacement** (per `SOV3_CLOUD_1_PAGER.md` non-goals)
- **Not a single-tenant on-prem product** (Enterprise tier's on-prem option is the only on-prem)
- **Not a marketplace** (the 6 channels are free + open; SOV3 Cloud is the paid SKU)
- **Not a managed service for the open-source flagships** (76 flagships stay MIT + self-host)
