## 6. The 7 writing rules

1. **Factual comparative, no war rhetoric** (per `RUBRIC_EXTERNAL_COMMS.md`). Compare features, never trash people.
2. **1-2 differentiators per post.** Do not try to ship all 8 in one post — focus beats breadth.
3. **Link to a source from `/tmp/kimi_dossier_v2/research/` in every post.** Verifiability is the differentiator.
4. **Open with a question or a number, not a claim.** "78% of EU enterprises are unprepared" > "We help you with EU AI Act."
5. **One CTA per post, not three.** Pick the highest-value conversion (free scanner > demo > pricing).
6. **Subject line / headline < 60 chars.** Twitter and HN punish verbosity.
7. **End with a verifiable artifact.** HMAC-signed attestation, GitHub link, MCP install command — never a marketing CTA.

## 7. The 4 "do NOT do" list (per the dossier's `RUBRIC_EXTERNAL_COMMS.md` + the audit's banned-vocabulary list)

- ❌ **No war vocabulary**: kill shot, nuclear arsenal, coup de grâce, talent raid, seeding doubt, depletion campaign, strike while, vulnerability window, acquisition target, funding fiction.
- ❌ **No specific-company failure references**: CrowdStrike BSOD, CISA exploited-vuln list, OneTrust layoffs, WitnessAI "no API."
- ❌ **No over-promising**: "MEOK ships in 48h" is true; "zero-config" is true ONLY for the EU AI Act wedge. Be precise.
- ❌ **No external TAM quotes**: never cite $1.2T TAM or $48M run-rate externally (per `sov3-mcp-master-audit-2026-06-08.md` memory's banned-vocabulary list). The $50B GRC no-MCP claim IS safe externally (factual market-structure fact).
