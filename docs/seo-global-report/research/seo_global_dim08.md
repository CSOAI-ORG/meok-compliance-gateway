## 1. Executive Summary

The Asia-Pacific (APAC) region represents the world's most diverse and complex search engine optimization landscape. Unlike Western markets where Google dominates uniformly, APAC requires distinct strategies for each market:

| Market | Primary Search Engine | Mobile Share | Key Differentiator |
|--------|----------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| China | Baidu (70%+ mobile) | 98%+ | ICP license required; WeChat ecosystem |
| South Korea | Naver (51-60%) | 95%+ | Naver Blog/Café/Knowledge iN ecosystem |
| Japan | Google (73%) + Yahoo! Japan (9-18%) | 60%+ | Multi-script keywords; formality levels |
| India | Google (98%) | 90%+ | Multilingual; voice search; mobile-first |
| Indonesia | Google (93-96%) | 90%+ | Bahasa Indonesia; local citations |
| Thailand | Google (dominant) + LINE | 90%+ | LINE app integration; social commerce |
| Vietnam | Google (dominant) + Cốc Cốc | 92%+ | Zalo integration; hyperlocal SEO |
| Australia | Google (94%) | 40%+ | Local SEO; GBP optimization |
| Singapore | Google (95%) | 66%+ | 4-language multilingual SEO |

**Key Finding:** APAC's digital economy exceeded $300B GMV in 2025, with projections of $1-2T by 2030. Video commerce accounts for 25-32% of e-commerce GMV. India alone has 700M+ smartphone users, making voice search optimization critical. [^34^][^250^][^282^]

## 2. Baidu SEO Optimization (China)

### 2.1 Market Context

- **Baidu Market Share:** 70%+ on mobile search in China; 84.97% mobile search market share
- **Key Competitors:** Bing China, Sogou, 360 Search
- **Unique Challenge:** Google is blocked by Great Firewall; Baidu operates in a closed ecosystem

### 2.2 Technical SEO Requirements

- **URL:** https://www.eliteasia.co/baidu-seo/
- **Date:** 2026-02-04
- **Excerpt:** "Baidu's crawlers prefer straightforward site structures and struggle more than Google with JavaScript-heavy sites, AJAX loading, and single-page applications."
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Baidu places significant weight on meta keywords tags (which Google ignores), and title tags should be 32-54 characters. [^183^]
- **Source:** Elite Asia Baidu SEO Guide
- **URL:** https://www.eliteasia.co/baidu-seo/
- **Date:** 2026-02-04
- **Excerpt:** "Meta keywords tags are completely ignored by Google but still factor into Baidu's ranking calculations. Title tag length limits are shorter (32-54 characters versus Google's 60)."
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Baidu's ERNIE language model surpasses Google's BERT in understanding Chinese language nuances. [^183^]
- **Source:** Elite Asia Baidu SEO Guide
- **URL:** https://www.eliteasia.co/baidu-seo/
- **Date:** 2026-02-04
- **Excerpt:** "ERNIE actually surpasses Google's technology in understanding Chinese language nuances, context, and semantic relationships."
- **Confidence:** High

### 13.2 Content Restrictions & Workarounds
