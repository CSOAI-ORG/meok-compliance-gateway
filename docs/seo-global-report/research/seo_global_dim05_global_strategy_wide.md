## Facet: Global SEO/GEO/AEO/SMM World Domination Strategy

### Key Findings

- **Global SEO is converging with GEO and AEO** into a unified "Search Everywhere Optimization" framework — SEO establishes baseline visibility, AEO ensures accessibility in answer-driven search features, and GEO positions content as trusted reference material for LLM-generated responses [^5^]
- **LATAM is NOT a single market** — it comprises at least 5 fundamentally different digital systems (Chile, Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Mexico) with dramatically different keyword stability periods (18+ months in Chile vs. 6-8 weeks in Argentina) and conversion rates (8.4% in Chile vs. 3.2% in Mexico) [^16^]
- **APAC search engine diversity** requires multi-platform strategies: Baidu dominates China (70%+), Naver commands South Korea (~42-70%), Yahoo! Japan maintains significant presence, while Google leads in Southeast Asia, India, and Australia [^34^][^37^]
- **Content localization outperforms translation by 40x** — a case study showed native keyword research + localized content achieved 2,000 organic visitors/month vs. 50 visitors/month for translated-only content in Germany [^55^]
- **ChatGPT reached 900 million weekly active users** by February 2026, with the US leading at ~15-17% of traffic, followed by India at ~8-16%, and Brazil at ~5% — making AI search optimization critical for global visibility [^118^][^121^][^122^]
- **Mobile-first is non-negotiable** in emerging markets — India has 80%+ mobile-first searches, Brazil 75%, and Africa's internet access is primarily mobile-driven [^55^][^36^]
- **Southeast Asia's digital economy** is on track to surpass $300 billion in GMV by 2025, and could triple to nearly $1 trillion by 2030 (or $2 trillion with DEFA completion) [^75^][^76^]
- **Only 12% of URLs cited by LLMs** also appear in Google's top 10 results, indicating that AI search visibility requires fundamentally different optimization strategies than traditional SEO [^48^]
- **The Princeton GEO Study** found that adding expert quotes boosts AI visibility by 41%, statistics by 30%, and inline citations by 30% — while keyword stuffing degrades visibility by 9% [^46^]
- **E-E-A-T signals don't travel internationally** — strong US PR doesn't help if there's zero local coverage in the target market; local backlinks, citations, and entity connections are essential per-region [^113^]
- **Voice search accounts for over 50% of online searches** by 2025, with multilingual voice search (Hindi, Spanish, Mandarin) growing exponentially [^81^][^77^]
- **Africa's digital economy is projected to reach $180 billion by 2027**, with over 500 million people online and Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa leading [^32^][^40^]

### GEO/AEO Multi-Language Optimization

**Core Framework:**
SEO, AEO, and GEO now operate as a complementary three-layer strategy [^5^]:
- **SEO**: Establishes baseline visibility across traditional search engines
- **AEO**: Ensures accessibility in answer-driven search features (AI Overviews, featured snippets)
- **GEO**: Positions content as trusted reference material for LLM-generated responses

**Key GEO Findings from Princeton Study:** [^46^]

| Tactic | Impact on AI Visibility |
|--------|------------------------|
| Adding expert quotes with attribution | +41% |
| Including clear statistics and data | +30% |
| Adding inline citations to sources | +30% |
| Improving readability and fluency | +22% |
| Using domain-specific terminology | +21% |
| Simplifying language | +15% |
| Authoritative voice and tone | +11% |
| Keyword stuffing | -9% |

**Multi-Language GEO/AEO Strategies:**

1. **Entity Consistency Across Languages** [^44^][^46^]
   - Maintain consistent brand name, descriptions, and facts across all language versions
   - Use schema markup (Organization, Person) in each language
   - Same facts must appear identically across website, GBP, Bing, Yelp, LinkedIn, Facebook

2. **Structured Content for Extraction** [^45^][^46^]
   - FAQPage and HowTo schema in each target language
   - Direct answers (40-60 words) under question headings
   - Tables, lists, and comparison formats for easy AI extraction
   - Answer-first strategy: answer the question immediately, then elaborate

3. **Cultural Schema Adaptation** [^84^]
   - Schema markup needs cultural adaptation, not direct translation
   - Business hours, service descriptions must reflect local practices
   - FAQ schema must use culturally appropriate question phrasing
   - Layer multiple schema types with language-specific variations

4. **Authority Building Per Language** [^112^][^113^]
   - Local expert authorship for each market
   - Country-specific backlinks (not just translated content)
   - Regional PR mentions and citations
   - Local case studies and testimonials
   - Strong U.S. PR doesn't help without local coverage in target market

5. **Content Structure for AI Visibility** [^48^]
   - Only 12% of URLs cited by LLMs also appear in Google's top 10
   - AI Overviews exception: 76% of cited URLs overlap with top 100 organic
   - Perplexity: 28% overlap with Google; ChatGPT: only 8% overlap
   - Multi-language FAQ sections with proper hreflang
   - Clear geographic and language intent signals

6. **Voice Search Multi-Language Optimization** [^77^][^81^][^84^]
   - Hindi, Spanish, Mandarin voice searches growing exponentially
   - Conversational keyword optimization in each language
   - Long-tail question phrases in natural language
   - Featured snippet targeting in each target language
   - Google Business Profile optimization for local voice queries

7. **Regional AI Search Platform Considerations:**
   - ChatGPT leads in most of APAC outside China [^37^]
   - Google AI Overviews visible across many APAC markets [^37^]
   - Baidu Ernie Bot critical in China [^37^]
   - Naver's AI gaining in South Korea [^34^]
