## Executive Summary

This deep-dive research covers competitive intelligence, backlink acquisition strategies, and entity SEO optimization for a portfolio of 25 .ai domains spanning AI governance, construction, pet care, legal tech, mental health, gaming, and productivity verticals. Key findings:

- **.ai TLD is SEO-neutral but brand-positive**: Google treats .ai as generic TLD (not geo-targeted to Anguilla), though user trust is 15% lower than .com [^546^]
- **Domain Authority timeline**: New sites require 6-12 months for meaningful DA movement; 5-10 point DA increases achievable in 6 months with consistent effort [^504^]
- **Link velocity benchmarks**: New sites should target 5-15 links/month; mid-authority sites 20-50/month; average across industries is 48 new referring domains/month [^520^][^519^]
- **Anchor text best practices**: 50-70% branded/URL anchors, 20-30% partial match, 5-10% exact match [^520^]
- **Editorial links dominate**: 92.2% of backlinks to top-ranking sites are editorial; directories account for only 6.8% [^519^]
- **HARO delivers high-value links**: 3-5 qualifying links/month expected at $975 for 3 links [^500^]
- **Multi-domain linking advantage**: Brands connecting domains strategically see 30-45% higher cumulative organic traffic [^113^]
- **Entity-first SEO is critical**: Proprietary knowledge graphs and structured data are prerequisites for AI search visibility [^476^]

## 14. Skyscraper Technique

### Key Principles (from established SEO best practices):
1. **Find link-worthy content** in your niche using Ahrefs Content Explorer
2. **Create something significantly better** - longer, more current, better designed
3. **Reach out to sites linking to original** content with personalized pitches
4. **Focus on content with proven link demand**, not speculative topics

### Best Content Types for Skyscraper in 2026:
- Original research and data studies
- Comprehensive industry benchmarks
- Interactive tools and calculators
- Ultimate guides with 5,000+ words
- Visual explainers and infographics
