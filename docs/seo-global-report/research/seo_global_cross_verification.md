## CONFLICT ZONES

### Conflict 1: Market Size Variance
- **AI Governance market**: Dim01 reports $940M (NextMSC) vs $492M (Gartner via Nemko) for 2025
  - Resolution: Different scope — NextMSC includes consulting services, Gartner is platform-only
  - Status: RESOLVED — both figures are correct for their respective scopes

### Conflict 2: Page-One Referring Domains
- **Dim12 reports median 907 referring domains for page-one rankings**
- **Dim03 notes RetroDodo (DR 47) drives 1M+ monthly clicks**
  - Resolution: 907 is median across ALL industries; niche sites with topical authority can rank with far fewer links
  - Status: RESOLVED — niche authority compensates for lower backlink counts

### Conflict 3: Content Update Frequency
- **Dim11**: Quarterly updates = 3x citation retention
- **Wide06**: Content freshness matters, but 3-month "citation cliff" varies by industry
  - Resolution: Update frequency should be industry-specific; fast-moving topics (AI governance) need monthly, stable topics (legal) can be quarterly
  - Status: RESOLVED — contextualized by industry velocity

### Conflict 4: SEO vs GEO Priority
- **Dim11 emphasizes GEO/AEO as primary focus**
- **Dim07, Dim12 maintain traditional SEO is still foundation**
  - Resolution: 99.5% of AI Overview sources come from top 10 organic — you need BOTH
  - Status: RESOLVED — integrated strategy required, not either/or
